Made for long books

A novel takes weeks. The player has to remember where you were, let you jump back a minute, and keep going while you do other things.

Your folders are the library

Drop folders or files onto the window. Rename books, make folders for genres or authors, and move things around inside the app. Covers and blurbs can be fetched from Audible, Apple Books or Open Library in a click.

Chapters, bookmarks, history

Chapter ticks on the seek bar, notes on bookmarks, and a history that undoes an accidental jump.

Speed, equalizer, sleep timer

Speed from 0.5x to 3x with pitch preserved, a five-band equalizer, volume boost, silence skipping, and a sleep timer that fades out and asks where you dozed off.

Part of the desktop

Closing the window keeps the book playing from the tray. Media keys, global hotkeys and taskbar controls all work, and playback pauses when your headphones unplug.

Series kept in order

Series are read from tags or folder names, the card shows "3 of 6", and the next book is offered the moment one ends.

Common questions

Where does my listening progress live?

In a small folder under your Windows user profile, as plain JSON files. Nothing leaves your PC. Delete the folder and Audlet starts fresh.

Do I need to install .NET first?

No. The installer carries everything it needs.

Does it play Audible files?

It plays open formats: MP3, M4A, M4B, FLAC, Opus, OGG, WAV and WavPack. Protected AAX files are not supported.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no trial, no subscription and no account.
